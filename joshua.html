Sources & method

The plotted sequence and names follow Joshua 1–12. Geographic placement was cross-checked against BibleMapper’s campaign maps, BiblePlaces’ Joshua 12 town study, and standard historical-geography identifications. Dashed marker outlines indicate especially uncertain locations.

  • Joshua 3:10: the seven peoples of Canaan
  • Leviticus 18:21–30; Deuteronomy 12:29–31 and 18:9–12: sexual sin, divination and child sacrifice among the nations
  • Numbers 13:29: Amalek in the Negeb and broad regional associations
  • Deuteronomy 2:9, 19: Moab and Ammon were not Israel’s territorial targets
  • Joshua 12:1–6: Sihon and Og east of the Jordan
  • Joshua 15:8, 63: Jerusalem as the Jebusite city
  • The Gospel Coalition: Deuteronomy course and TGC Bible Commentaries
  • Bible.org / NET Bible: Baalism in Canaanite Religion and NET Bible study notes
  • Ligonier Ministries: Reformed Bible studies on Baal and Asherah
  • Associates for Biblical Research: archaeological articles on Canaanite religion
  • BibleMapper and BiblePlaces: campaign and location cross-checks

This is a teaching visualization, not a claim that every archaeological identification or every people’s distinct cult is certain.
