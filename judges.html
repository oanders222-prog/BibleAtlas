Sources & method

The seven-cycle structure follows the conservative evangelical outline presented by Bible.org: Othniel, Ehud, Shamgar, Deborah and Barak, Gideon, Jephthah, and Samson. Ligonier’s overview classifies six as major judges and Shamgar as a minor judge, but recognizes his literary role alongside the early deliverers.

  • Primary text: Judges 2:6–16:31
  • John Currid, Judges, The Gospel Coalition Bible Commentary
  • Robert D. Bell, “Three Things You Should Know About Judges,” Ligonier Ministries
  • Bible.org / NET Bible, Israel’s Dark Ages, The Historical Books, and NET study notes
  • ESV and NKJV passage cross-checks
  • BibleMapper and standard biblical atlases for geographic cross-checking
